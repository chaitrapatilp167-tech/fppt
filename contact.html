Help us get you an answer faster

When you email or call, share the details that match your question:

  • Order or delivery: your order number and the email address used at checkout.
  • Return or exchange: your order number, item name, and whether you want a return or a different size.
  • Product question: the product name and the detail you’d like help with, such as fit, fabric, or care.
  • Repair: the item or order number and a short description of the damage. Use the repair request form to submit the details.

Need a garment repair?

Stackly offers complimentary lifetime repairs for its pieces. Use our repair form or email us with your order number, item, and a description of the repair needed. Most repairs take 14 business days after the garment reaches our atelier; complex repairs may take up to 21 business days.

Helpful information

What should I include when contacting customer care?
For an order question, include your order number and the email address used at checkout. For a product question, include the item name. For a repair request, use the repair form and describe the issue with the garment.
How long do repairs take?
Most repairs are completed within 14 business days after the atelier receives the garment. Complex repairs can take up to 21 days. Stackly provides a prepaid label and covers return shipping for eligible lifetime repairs.
Where are Stackly pieces made?
Stackly garments are designed in London and produced by specialist family-run ateliers in Biella, Italy; Porto, Portugal; and Okayama, Japan.
