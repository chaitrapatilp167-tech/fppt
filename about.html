Our Philosophy

Considered Clothing, Built to Endure

Founded in London, Stackly was born out of a desire to create an antidote to fast fashion — pieces made exclusively from natural fibres, crafted by master artisans.

A Note From Our Founder

"We measure success not by how many pieces we sell, but by how long they remain in your wardrobe."

When we launched Stackly in 2018, we set a strict rule: if a garment cannot be repaired, resoled, or biodegrade back into the earth at the end of its life, we do not make it.

We work directly with heritage mills across Europe and Asia, cutting out intermediaries so we can invest in superior craftsmanship while maintaining fair prices. We believe that truly luxurious clothing should be lived in, repaired, and passed down.

— Eleanor Vance, Founder & Creative Director

Uncompromising Quality

Crafted by Masters of the Trade

From the spinning of virgin wool in Biella, Italy, to the slow weaving of 14oz selvedge denim in Okayama, Japan, every Stackly garment is a testament to human skill.

We partner with family-owned ateliers who have honed their craft for generations. This deep respect for tradition results in clothing that not only looks exceptional but grows richer with time.

The Fabric of our Lives

Sourcing the World's Finest Materials

Our design process always begins with the fabric. Before a single sketch is drawn, our team spends months sourcing and developing bespoke textiles that meet our rigorous standards for durability, drape, and environmental impact.

We believe that luxury is tactile. Whether it's the crisp structure of our heavy-weight organic poplin or the buttery softness of our recycled cashmere blends, you can feel the Stackly difference the moment you touch our garments.

Our Commitment to the Earth

Designing for Circularity

We use exclusively natural, biodegradable fibres—such as certified organic cotton, recycled cashmere, and responsibly sourced linen. Synthetic plastics like polyester and nylon are completely banned from our collections, ensuring our garments never release microplastics into our oceans.

Beyond our materials, our facilities operate on 100% renewable energy, and we offset all shipping emissions to remain a carbon-neutral company. When you are done with a Stackly piece, our take-back program ensures it is recycled into new yarn.

Our Atelier Locations

Biella, Italy

Virgin Wool & Cashmere Spinning

Home to the world's finest wool processing. Water from the Alps naturally washes the fibres, producing incomparable softness and durability for our knitwear.

Porto, Portugal

Leather Goods & Footwear

Third-generation cobblers hand-stitch our Goodyear-welted leather Chelsea boots and vegetable-tanned leather totes using traditional techniques.

Okayama, Japan

Selvedge Denim Weaving

Vintage Toyoda shuttle looms weave our 14oz rigid denim at slow speeds for maximum strength, durability, and a unique character that fades beautifully.
