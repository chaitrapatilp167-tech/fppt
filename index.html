Our story

Clothes that earn their place

Stackly began with a simple frustration: too many clothes, made too quickly, designed to be replaced. We set out to build a different kind of wardrobe — one built around quality over quantity.

Every piece starts with the material. We source natural fibres from established mills in Italy, Japan, and Scotland — suppliers who have been perfecting their craft for decades, sometimes centuries.

Need answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are Stackly garments manufactured?
All Stackly garments are designed in London and produced in family-run ateliers in Biella (Italy), Porto (Portugal), and Okayama (Japan). Every factory adheres to fair-wage labor standards.
How does Stackly's Free Lifetime Repair service work?
If a Stackly garment ever needs seam re-stitching, button replacement, or lining touch-ups, send it to our studio. We will repair it free of charge to keep it in your rotation forever.
What is Stackly's return policy?
We offer free returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached.
How should I care for pure wool and silk pieces?
Wool overcoats and knitwear should be dry cleaned or hand-washed in cold water using a wool detergent, then dried flat. Silk pieces are machine washable on a delicate cold cycle inside a garment bag.
