Maintenance Guide

How to Care for Stackly

Proper care is the foundation of sustainability. We recommend washing less, airing out more, and following these specific guidelines for our signature natural fibres.

🧶

Wool & Cashmere

Our heavy-weight wool and recycled cashmere are naturally antibacterial and stain-resistant. They require very little washing.

  • Air out garments between wears rather than frequent washing to restore freshness.
  • Hand wash cold only when necessary, using a pH-neutral wool detergent.
  • Never wring or twist. Gently press the water out and dry flat on a clean towel.
  • Use a cashmere comb gently on areas of high friction to remove natural pilling.
✨

Mulberry Silk

Our silk is exceptionally durable when treated with care. It is highly breathable and regulates body temperature beautifully.

  • Machine wash inside a delicate laundry bag on a cold, delicate cycle.
  • Use a specialist silk detergent to preserve the natural sheen of the fibres.
  • Never wring silk; instead, hang it to air dry away from direct sunlight.
  • Steam or iron on the coolest setting while the fabric is still slightly damp.
👞

Full-Grain Leather

Our Portuguese vegetable-tanned leather will develop a rich, unique patina over time. Regular conditioning is the key to longevity.

  • Wipe clean with a damp cloth to remove daily dust and dirt.
  • Apply an organic beeswax leather conditioner every 3 months to prevent drying out.
  • Insert cedar shoe trees into footwear immediately after every wear to maintain shape.
  • Allow wet leather to dry naturally away from radiators or direct heat sources.

Repair Service FAQ

What does the lifetime guarantee cover?
Our lifetime repair guarantee covers all natural wear and tear. This includes dropped hems, unravelled seams, lost buttons, broken zippers, and lining replacements. It does not cover complete structural alterations (like resizing) or extreme damage outside of normal wear.
How long does a repair take?
Most repairs are completed within 14 business days from the moment our atelier receives your garment. Complex lining replacements or major zipper work may take up to 21 days. We will notify you via email as soon as it ships back.
Do I have to pay for shipping?
No. Once you submit a repair request, we will email you a pre-paid printable shipping label. We also cover the cost of shipping the repaired garment back to you.
